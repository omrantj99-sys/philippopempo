1Data we collect

  • Account: email address, name and profile photo. With Google Sign-In we receive only your email, name and profile picture (basic scopes: openid, email, profile). With Telegram sign-in we receive your Telegram user ID and display name.
  • Password: if you set a password, it is handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash. We never see or store it in readable form.
  • Phone number: entered by you when you complete your account, so friends can find you in the app.
  • Content: text messages, images, voice messages, PDF files (up to 10 MB each, visible to the members of the chat they are sent in), polls and votes, scheduled messages (kept until they are sent), saved messages, replies and reactions, statuses (which expire after 24 hours), and Reels (short videos with an optional caption, likes, comments and reports). Reels are visible to all users of the app and expire after 7 days.
  • Study rooms: when you create or join a study room we store its 6-character code, who the members are, and the shared 30-minute timer state (start and end time). Each room has a linked group chat, so its messages and call metadata are stored like any other group chat. Anyone who has a room’s code can join the room and its chat, so share the code only with people you trust. A room’s host can appoint moderators, and the host or a moderator can remove a member, in which case we keep a record of that removal so the person cannot rejoin the same room.
  • Study time and leaderboard: we record completed study sessions (when they ran and which room members took part) to calculate your study time, which only you can see. Your name, profile photo and study hours appear on the public leaderboard only if you turn on “Show me on the public leaderboard”; it is off by default and you can turn it off at any time.
  • AI study assistant: when you use “Ask the assistant” in a chat, the text of your question is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to generate an answer. The question and the answer are then posted in that chat as a normal message, visible to its members. We also keep a daily usage counter per account to enforce a daily limit. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your questions.
  • Activity: last-seen time (you can hide it in “My account”), read receipts, and call metadata (who called, when, and call status).
  • Notifications: if you enable notifications, we store the device subscription data needed to deliver them.
  • Calls: we do not record or store the content of voice or video calls; it is relayed in real time between participants only.

2How we use data

Only to operate the app: sign-in and account recovery, delivering messages, calls and notifications, and showing statuses to people you share a chat with.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3Service providers

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage.
  • LiveKit: real-time audio/video for calls.
  • Google: sign-in, Google Fonts (which may receive your IP address when loading the font), and the Gemini API that generates the study assistant’s answers. Google processes that text under its own terms, which may allow it to use content sent through free API tiers to improve its services.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the app pages.
  • Browser/OS push services (Google, Apple, Mozilla): delivering notifications.
  • Telegram: sign-in through our bot, and delivery of one-time codes for password recovery.
  • Gmail: sending email login and password-recovery codes.

4Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider, and is protected by access rules that prevent users from reading chats they are not part of.

Chats are not currently end-to-end encrypted, so the service operator can technically access stored data; this is done only for maintenance or when legally required. We plan to introduce end-to-end encryption in the future.

5Retention and deletion

  • Statuses are deleted automatically after 24 hours, and Reels after 7 days.
  • You can delete your own Reels at any time, and any user can report a Reel for review.
  • You can delete your own messages for everyone inside the app.
  • You can block other users from the chat screen.
  • You can leave a study room at any time, which removes you from the room and its group chat. Deleting your account also removes your study-room memberships and your study-time records.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app (My account → Delete account).
  • You can also contact us using the details below, and we will complete the request within 30 days.

6Your rights

You may access, correct and delete your data, hide your last seen, change your password, and disable notifications at any time.

7Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the “Last updated” date above.

For questions or privacy requests, open an issue at github.com/omrantj99-sys/philippopempo (please do not post private details; we will arrange verification privately).
