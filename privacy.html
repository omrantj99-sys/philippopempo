PhilippoPempo ← Back to app Last updated: November 22, 2026 Privacy Policy PhilippoPempo is a messaging web app (PWA) at omrantj99-sys.github.io/philippopempo. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it. 🚫 No ads We never show ads. 📵 No tracking No analytics or tracking tools. 🤝 Never sold We do not sell your data. 🗑️ You’re in control Delete your account any time. Contents 1 Data we collect 2 How we use data 3 Service providers 4 Security 5 Retention and deletion 6 Your rights 7 Children 8 Changes and contact 1 Data we collect Account: email address, name and profile photo. With Google Sign-In we receive only your email, name and profile picture (basic scopes: openid, email, profile). With Telegram sign-in we receive your Telegram user ID and display name. Password: if you set a password, it is handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash. We never see or store it in readable form. Phone number: entered by you when you complete your account, so friends can find you in the app. Content: text messages, images, voice messages, polls and votes, scheduled messages (kept until they are sent), saved messages, replies and reactions, statuses (which expire after 24 hours), and Reels (short videos with an optional caption, likes, comments and reports). Reels are visible to all users of the app and expire after 7 days. Activity: last-seen time (you can hide it in “My account”), read receipts, and call metadata (who called, when, and call status). Notifications: if you enable notifications, we store the device subscription data needed to deliver them. Calls: we do not record or store the content of voice or video calls; it is relayed in real time between participants only. 2 How we use data Only to operate the app: sign-in and account recovery, delivering messages, calls and notifications, and showing statuses to people you share a chat with. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. 3 Service providers Supabase: database, authentication and file storage. LiveKit: real-time audio/video for calls. Google: sign-in, and Google Fonts (which may receive your IP address when loading the font). GitHub Pages: hosting the app pages. Browser/OS push services (Google, Apple, Mozilla): delivering notifications. Telegram: sign-in through our bot, and delivery of one-time codes for password recovery. Gmail: sending email login and password-recovery codes. 4 Security Data is transmitted over HTTPS, is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider, and is protected by access rules that prevent users from reading chats they are not part of. Chats are not currently end-to-end encrypted, so the service operator can technically access stored data; this is done only for maintenance or when legally required. We plan to introduce end-to-end encryption in the future. 5 Retention and deletion Statuses are deleted automatically after 24 hours, and Reels after 7 days. You can delete your own Reels at any time, and any user can report a Reel for review. You can delete your own messages for everyone inside the app. You can block other users from the chat screen. You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app (My account → Delete account). You can also contact us using the details below, and we will complete the request within 30 days. 6 Your rights You may access, correct and delete your data, hide your last seen, change your password, and disable notifications at any time. 7 Children The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. 8 Changes and contact We may update this policy and will change the “Last updated” date above. For questions or privacy requests, open an issue at github.com/omrantj99-sys/philippopempo (please do not post private details; we will arrange verification privately). © PhilippoPempo · Back to the app
